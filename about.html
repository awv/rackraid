About the Archive

Inside the Historical Course Guide & Analytics Engine

The Project

The Rack Raid Archive is a dedicated historical repository built to preserve, standardise, and analyse running datasets from the traditional 14-stage regional relay. By collecting data tracking thousands of individual historical performances spanning from 2011 to 2026, this archive acts as an interactive hall of fame.

Whether you are researching an all-time course benchmark, reviewing your club's historical footprint, or tracking your own event longevity over the years, the platform dynamically groups rows, calculates multi-era running metrics, and visualises completion trends to bring regional racing history to life.

About the Author

This analytical archive engine was built and is maintained by a proud member of **Parc Bryn Bach Running Club**.

When not diving into messy relational data rows or mapping historic course variations, you will likely spot me out along the course loops—lurking around the various stages with a camera, capturing race action and photographing the runners as they tackle the circuit.

⚠️ Data Accuracy & Historic Anomalies

The metrics presented within this platform are aggregated completely from historical race results spreadsheets compiled over several decades. While the dataset is largely accurate and structurally sound, it may contain historical clerical mistakes, missing entries, or layout formatting variants inherited from the original source files.

To maintain historical transparency, structural data anomalies (such as missing runner classifications, blank fields, or placeholder values) have been programmatically bypassed or filtered where appropriate to preserve the integrity of the master all-time leaderboard rankings.
